MAXCOM R2, external camera guide

An external camera for live broadcasts

This guide is for musalla committee members. It explains how to set up a surveillance camera (a network camera) so that its picture can be used in a MAXCOM live broadcast, and how to manage its sound. Time needed: about 30 minutes the first time.

The labels below are those of the MAX Admin Client in English.

1. Settings on the camera

Open the camera's settings page on a computer (its address is in the camera manual). Set the following:

  1. Video codec: H.264 or H.265. H.264 is the simplest and the safest.
  2. Resolution: 1080p (1920 x 1080).
  3. Keyframe interval: every 2 seconds. If the camera asks for an "I-frame interval", set it to twice the frame rate (for example 50 if the camera runs at 25 frames a second).
  4. Fixed IP address: give the camera an address that does not change, for example 10.0.0.50, outside the range the router hands out to phones and computers. If the address changes, the camera will no longer be found.
  5. A view-only account: create one account just for MAXCOM that can only view, for example lihat ("view"). Do not give the camera's administrator account to MAXCOM.
  6. If the camera has its own microphone, turn its audio on and choose a common audio codec (AAC or G.711).

2. Check the camera stream on a computer first

Before using MAXCOM, make sure the camera really sends the right picture.

  1. On a computer on the same network as the camera, open a video player application.
  2. Choose the menu item for opening a network stream.
  3. Type the camera's stream address. It looks like rtsp://lihat:password@10.0.0.50:554/stream1. The last part of the address differs from camera to camera; see the camera manual.
  4. Press play. The picture should appear within a few seconds, smoothly, with sound if the camera has a microphone.
  5. If there is no picture, check the IP address, the account name and the password, and make sure the computer and the camera are on the same network.

Only when this step works, enter the same address in MAXCOM.

3. Broadcast sound

By default, the broadcast sound is the camera's own sound. The camera's picture and sound stay together, so they are always in step.

If the camera's sound is not clear enough (the camera is far from the speaker), press Add audio and choose another source:

  • This phone microphone, or This iPad microphone.
  • Mic-in on the box (a microphone plugged straight into the MAXCOM box).

Only one sound is on at a time. The source you add is turned on and the other rows are turned off. If a source is not available, it shows in grey with the reason, for example "This box has no audio input".

The Audio card shows one row for every sound input: External camera audio, This phone microphone (or This iPad microphone), External microphone, Bluetooth microphone and Mic-in on the box. Each row has Use this source, Volume, Mute this source and Lift quiet sound. A row that is off stays in the list, marked Not used, and can be turned on again. While the live is on air, changing the sound first asks "Change the sound? The broadcast pauses for a moment (2 to 3 seconds)." with Change and Cancel.

4. The volume of the sound

Each audio source has one row with a sound level meter and a Volume slider.

  1. Start with the sound that is on at normal volume.
  2. Ask someone to speak from the speaker's place.
  3. Watch the level meter. It should move when there is a voice, but not sit at full all the time.
  4. If the sound is noisy or far away (often the camera microphone), turn down its volume, or turn on a source that is closer to the speaker: the other rows are then turned off.
  5. Do not turn the sound that is on down to 0 or mute it. The broadcast would be silent and the screen shows a warning.

The last volume settings are remembered for the next broadcast.

5. Sound and picture stay together

MAXCOM joins the sound to the picture with a fixed delay. There is nothing to set and nothing to measure for the sound delay.

6. Sound from an iPad or Mic-in arrives 1 to 2 seconds late

When the sound of an iPad or of Mic-in on the box is used, the broadcast on the TV and on YouTube runs about 1 to 2 seconds behind what really happens in the musalla. This is normal and does not harm the broadcast. Do not be surprised if the TV in the musalla looks slightly behind the speaker.

7. Where to put the phone or iPad

If a phone or iPad microphone is the sound on air:

  1. Put the device close to the speaker, for example on a table near the minbar, with the microphone facing the speaker.
  2. Keep it away from the loudspeakers so that it does not howl.
  3. Leave the device's screen on throughout the broadcast. If the app is closed or the device goes to sleep, the microphone sound stops.
  4. Make sure the device is charged and does not take calls during the broadcast.

8. Cameras without sound

Some cameras have no microphone. The Audio card then says "This camera has no audio". Press Add audio and choose the microphone you will use (section 3), or the broadcast has no sound.

A camera with a microphone does not need this step.

9. External microphones

For clearer sound, use a microphone close to the speaker rather than relying on a camera microphone on the ceiling.

  • A microphone plugged into the MAXCOM box shows as Mic-in on the box, if that box has an audio input. A box without an audio input shows the reason.
  • The microphone of a phone or iPad placed close to the speaker (see section 7).
  • Some cameras take an external microphone directly. If so, plug the microphone into the camera and its sound becomes the camera's sound.

A USB or Lightning microphone on an iPad, or a USB-C microphone on an Android phone, is picked up by itself: the first time it is plugged in, the app uses it straight away and shows "New microphone: (name). Used for the live." with an Undo button. If it is unplugged, the broadcast goes back to the device microphone without stopping. Bluetooth microphones are not chosen automatically.

After changing microphones, check the Volume of each source again (section 4).

The camera's RTSP address, user name and password can be changed away from the surau. The app sends them to the box encrypted, and the box uses them, usually within 2 minutes. Find cameras (ONVIF) and Test connection work on the surau Wi-Fi only. The camera password is kept on the box only, never in the cloud.

10. Recording to the camera's microSD card as a backup

Most cameras can record to their own microSD card. Turn this on as a backup:

  1. Put a suitable microSD card in the camera.
  2. On the camera's settings page, turn recording on and choose continuous or scheduled recording.
  3. Format the card once from the camera's settings page before use.
  4. Check the card every month: the date of the last recording must be recent and the card must not be full.

If the network drops or the broadcast stops, the full recording of the event is still on the card. Download the files from it through the camera's page on a computer.

11. Camera control and picture brightness

The live settings are on the Live broadcast page, in the Live settings card. The video is chosen in one place, the Video source card: Phone camera (iPad camera on an iPad) or External camera. With the external camera, Video is taken by shows This phone or MAXCOM Kiosk Box.

Camera control. If the camera supports control over the network, an Admin turns on Allow camera control in the camera settings. The Live broadcast page then has a Camera control card with Brightness, Position with Home, Auto tracking (Off or Person) and Focus (Continuous, Single or Manual).

  1. Change the settings you want.
  2. Tap Apply camera settings to send them to the camera.
  3. The app shows "Command sent to the camera", then "The camera answered". If it does not arrive: "The command did not reach the camera".

With Apply when the live starts turned on, the same settings are sent every time a live starts.

Picture brightness. The choices are Automatic, Normal, Bright and Brighter. Automatic brightens the picture only when it is dark, such as at dusk. Choose Bright or Brighter if the picture is always too dark. It works when the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video from the camera.

When the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video, the live carries on even if the phone is locked or the app is left (see section 12).

12. When the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video

If Video is taken by is MAXCOM Kiosk Box, the Box reads the camera itself. The phone or iPad only controls the live and does not show the video: "The picture is not shown on this device. Watch it on the TV or on YouTube." This section explains what is different.

The live runs until Stop

This kind of live does not end by itself. It carries on when the phone or iPad is locked, when the app is left, when the phone loses its network, and when the camera drops: the TVs show the waiting card, and the picture and sound return by themselves when the camera connects again. The live stops only when someone taps Stop: the person who started it, other users who have live control, or an Admin. If the device that started the live has not been seen for a few minutes, other devices show the notice "The broadcast is still running, no device is in control" with a Stop the broadcast button.

Broadcast destinations

Before Go live and during the live, the Broadcast destinations sheet has the switches Screen, YouTube and Record. At least one destination must be on. YouTube can be turned on during a live but cannot be turned off. The details are in the Live Broadcast role guide.

Sound

  • The main sound is the MAXCOM microphone (main), the microphone connected to the Box (mic-in), at volume 100. This differs from section 3: the camera sound is no longer the main sound.
  • This microphone has its own sound path on the Box, so the sound stays smooth when the camera or the network stutters.
  • The camera sound (the row External camera audio) is listed but is not on air. To use the sound of the hall instead, turn that row on: the MAXCOM microphone is then turned off.
  • MAXCOM joins the sound to the picture with a fixed delay. There is nothing to set for the sound delay.

Automatic quality on YouTube

When the upload to YouTube is slow, MAXCOM lowers the picture quality sent to YouTube (480p) so that the broadcast does not cut, and returns to the normal quality by itself when the internet recovers. On an Android MAXCOM Kiosk Box the quality is not lowered: the broadcast is kept in a buffer instead and the notice "Upload to YouTube is slow" is shown.

Notices

  • "The camera picture is slow": a camera app on a phone or iPad becomes a second reader, and two readers drop the picture to 4 to 6 frames a second. Close the camera app during the broadcast.
  • "No sound from the MAXCOM microphone": check the microphone cable in the MAXCOM mic-in socket, or tap Use the camera sound. After about 90 seconds of silence the camera sound takes over by itself ("Camera sound in use").
  • "The MAXCOM microphone sound is breaking up": check that the cable is firm, or turn on the row External camera audio.
  • "The MAXCOM microphone is paused for now, the camera sound is used": the microphone on the Box stalled or was unplugged. An unplugged microphone is noticed within about 10 seconds and the camera sound is used until it returns. The broadcast is not silent. The microphone is tried again by itself, after 1 minute and then after longer waits (up to 5 minutes).
  • "Upload to YouTube is slow" and "Picture quality lowered (480p)": the upload from the musalla is slow. The broadcast carries on and the quality returns by itself. If it happens every time, check the internet at the musalla.
  • "The connection to YouTube was resumed n times": MAXCOM connected to YouTube again by itself. Information only.

Operations tips

  • Connect the MAXCOM Kiosk Box with a LAN cable where possible, not Wi-Fi.
  • Give the camera steady power. An unsteady supply makes the camera drop.
  • Close the camera maker's app on every phone and tablet during a live.
