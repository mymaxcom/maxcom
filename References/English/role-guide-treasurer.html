MAXCOM R2, role guide

Role guide: Treasurer

For the person who looks after the money of the musalla, for example the Treasurer of Surau Contoh. You record collections and payments, correct mistakes and prepare the monthly report. The full steps are in the user manual, Chapter 9.

1. What this role is for

The Treasurer role is for Finance and the musalla funds (Tabung). You see the Dashboard and the Finance menu. You can look at Content and Configuration, but not change them. All other menus are hidden from you.

2. Join with the QR code

You join the musalla on your own phone. You join with a QR code made by an Admin of Surau Contoh (in Bahasa Melayu the button is Daftar peranti).

  1. Ask an Admin for a QR code. The Admin makes it in Users & devices on the musalla Wi-Fi. A code is valid for 10 minutes by default; if it has expired, ask for a new one.
  2. Install the MAX Admin Client on your phone (download it from mymaxcom.my, see Chapter 2.1 of the user manual) and open it. Choose the language with BM | EN | ع if you need to.
  3. Tap Scan the QR code and scan the code on the Admin's phone. If the camera cannot be used, tap Enter the code manually and paste the enrolment code.
  4. Check your name and the musalla name (for example Surau Contoh) on the Check step.
  5. Fill in Device name, for example "Ustaz Contoh's phone".
  6. Set a PIN if the app asks for one, then tap Enrol device. The app shows "Device enrolled" and then the Dashboard.

3. Open the app

  • The App lock is set by an Admin. When it is off (the usual case), MAXCOM opens straight away on your phone. When it is on, you set a 6-digit PIN when you enrol (not all the same digit and not a sequence), and the app asks for it again after 2 hours unused, or after 12 hours. On Android a fingerprint can be used.
  • To change the PIN: More > This device > Change PIN. If you forgot it, tap Forgot your PIN? and ask an Admin to reset it (Reset PIN).
  • After several wrong PINs the app shows "PIN locked for now". Wait for the time shown, or ask an Admin to unlock you.
  • The first screen is the Dashboard. The bottom bar shows your main menus and the others are under More. A menu you have no permission for is not shown.

4. Finance: your screens

Open Finance. The page shows Total of all funds, In this month, Out this month, Latest entries and the buttons Collection, Payment, Correction and Monthly report. The message Saved on this device means the entry is safe on your phone even without a connection, and it is sent to MAXCOM later.

Record a collection

  1. Tap Collection.
  2. Choose the Date (Today or Last Friday), the Fund, the Purpose and the How received.
  3. Fill in the Amount. For cash you can tap Count by notes and coins and choose Counted by (2 people).
  4. Add a Photo of the count sheet if you like, tap Review and save, then Save. The app shows a reference number.

Once saved, an entry cannot be changed. A mistake is fixed with a Correction.

Record a payment

  1. Tap Payment. Choose Speaker (pick a kuliah) or Other spending.
  2. Choose Paid from (the fund), fill in the Amount, Paid to and How paid.
  3. Add a Receipt or invoice if there is one, then save. The app shows the fund balance before and after.

Make a correction

  1. Tap Correction and find the entry by number, fund or purpose.
  2. Choose the Kind of correction: Adjust amount, Cancel entry or Change fund.
  3. Write the Reason for the correction (at least 15 characters) and tap Save correction. The original entry stays, locked, and Entry history shows every correction.

Monthly report

  1. Tap Monthly report and choose the month. The status is Draft or Final.
  2. Tap Download PDF, Share or Print.

The Finance audit history lists who did what and when. It is explained in Chapter 9.6 of the user manual.

5. What you cannot do

Ask an Admin for these (the app does not show them, or shows them for viewing only):

  • Set up the funds: add, rename or archive a fund, the collection and payment categories, the opening balances (Fund settings).
  • Close or reopen a month (Close the month (Final)).
  • Add or change content (Content is for viewing only), change the settings in Configuration, or manage users and devices.
  • Go live: the Live broadcast menu belongs to the Live role.
  • An entry for a closed month is refused. Ask an Admin to reopen the month.

6. If something is wrong

  • I cannot see the Finance menu: menus depend on your roles and on whether the Finance feature is on. Ask an Admin to check your roles.
  • I saved a wrong amount: it cannot be edited. Use Correction.
  • The enrolment code has expired: ask the Admin for a new code.
  • I forgot my PIN, or the PIN is locked: see section 3, or ask an Admin to unlock you in Users & devices.
  • My phone is lost: tell an Admin at once. The Admin deletes that device so it can no longer unlock.
  • My change is not on the TV yet: look at the cloud and box icons in the top bar. If changes are waiting, tap Sync now. From home it can take a few minutes.
  • Still stuck: write to admin@mymaxcom.my.
