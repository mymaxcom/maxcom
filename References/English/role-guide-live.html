MAXCOM R2, role guide

Role guide: Live Broadcast

For the person who starts the live broadcast of the kuliah, for example the volunteer of Surau Contoh who runs the phone or camera. You start and stop the live, set the camera, the sound and the recording, and share the live link on WhatsApp. The full steps are in the user manual, Chapter 8.

1. What this role is for

The Live Broadcast role (Siaran Langsung in Bahasa Melayu) opens the Live broadcast menu and its settings: external camera, audio sources, screen, YouTube and recording. You see the Dashboard, the Live broadcast menu and YouTube upload. You can look at Configuration but not change it. All other menus are hidden from you.

2. Join with the QR code

You join the musalla on your own phone or iPad. You join with a QR code made by an Admin of Surau Contoh (in Bahasa Melayu the button is Daftar peranti).

  1. Ask an Admin for a QR code. The Admin makes it in Users & devices on the musalla Wi-Fi. A code is valid for 10 minutes by default; if it has expired, ask for a new one.
  2. Install the MAX Admin Client on your phone (download it from mymaxcom.my, see Chapter 2.1 of the user manual) and open it. Choose the language with BM | EN | ع if you need to.
  3. Tap Scan the QR code and scan the code on the Admin's phone. If the camera cannot be used, tap Enter the code manually and paste the enrolment code.
  4. Check your name and the musalla name (for example Surau Contoh) on the Check step.
  5. Fill in Device name, for example "Ustaz Contoh's phone".
  6. Set a PIN if the app asks for one, then tap Enrol device. The app shows "Device enrolled" and then the Dashboard.

3. Open the app

  • The App lock is set by an Admin. When it is off (the usual case), MAXCOM opens straight away on your phone. When it is on, you set a 6-digit PIN when you enrol (not all the same digit and not a sequence), and the app asks for it again after 2 hours unused, or after 12 hours. On Android a fingerprint can be used.
  • To change the PIN: More > This device > Change PIN. If you forgot it, tap Forgot your PIN? and ask an Admin to reset it (Reset PIN).
  • After several wrong PINs the app shows "PIN locked for now". Wait for the time shown, or ask an Admin to unlock you.
  • The first screen is the Dashboard. The bottom bar shows your main menus and the others are under More. A menu you have no permission for is not shown.

If the live uses the camera of the phone or iPad itself, keep the screen on and the device charged: locking the screen stops the camera. If the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video, the live does not stop when the phone is locked (see section 4).

4. Live broadcast: your screens

Start and stop a live

  1. Open Live broadcast. Choose a kuliah with Choose a kuliah, or No kuliah.
  2. Under Video source, choose Phone camera (iPad camera on an iPad) or External camera. With the external camera, Video is taken by shows This phone or MAXCOM Kiosk Box.
  3. Check the destinations right above the button: Screen, YouTube and Record. Tap any of them to open the Broadcast destinations sheet and turn each destination on or off (see "Broadcast destinations" below). At least one destination must be on.
  4. Tap Go live. The app shows On air, the time, and "To the surau screen and YouTube" or "To the surau screen only".
  5. To end, tap Stop, then Stop to confirm. If the connection drops, the app says "The broadcast has not stopped yet. Try again." Tap Try again until it stops.

If YouTube is chosen but cannot be used, the app asks "Start the broadcast to the surau screen only?". Tap Yes for the musalla screen only, or Cancel. If the live started for the musalla only, tap Broadcast on YouTube, fill in the Title on YouTube and the Visibility, then tap Broadcast now.

Live settings (external camera, audio, screen, YouTube, recording)

All the live settings are in the Live settings card on the Live broadcast page: External camera, Live on the screen, YouTube live and Recording. In Configuration only one pointer row is left.

  • External camera: turn on Use external camera, fill in the Camera name, tap Find cameras on the network (or type the RTSP address), the camera User name and Password, tap Test connection, then Save. If the camera supports control, the Camera control card has Brightness, Position and Focus; tap Apply camera settings to send them. Picture brightness has four choices: Automatic, Normal, Bright and Brighter.
  • Audio: each sound input has its own row with Use this source, Volume, Mute this source or Unmute this source and Lift quiet sound. To add a microphone, tap Add audio. Do not turn every source down to 0, or the broadcast is silent. A new plugged-in microphone is picked up by itself; tap Undo to go back.
  • YouTube: the musalla's channel is connected once, on the musalla Wi-Fi, by an Admin or by you (the Live Broadcast role can sign in to YouTube). You can set the YouTube visibility, Title template and Description template in the live settings.
  • Video source: Video is taken by shows This phone or MAXCOM Kiosk Box; with MAXCOM Kiosk Box the phone only controls the live and does not show the video (see "A live whose video is taken by the MAXCOM Kiosk Box" below).

Broadcast destinations: Screen, YouTube and Record

Right above the Go live button there are three chips: Screen, YouTube and Record. Tap any of them to open the Broadcast destinations sheet, which has one switch for each destination. The choice applies to every live, including a live from the camera of the phone or iPad itself.

  • Change the switches before you tap Go live, or during the live, until you tap Stop.
  • At least one destination must be on. If all are off, Go live cannot be tapped and the app says "Choose at least one destination". During a live, the last destination that is still on cannot be turned off: "The last destination cannot be turned off during a broadcast".
  • Screen is the surau screen (the TVs). It starts on and your choice is remembered for the next live. When it is off, the TVs do not show the live and the app says "The surau screens are off for this broadcast".
  • YouTube can be turned on during a live but cannot be turned off: "Can be turned on during a broadcast, cannot be turned off". Yes or No is a choice for that MAXCOM Kiosk Box and every person who runs lives sees the same.
  • Record starts according to the current recording setting. Turning it off during a live stops the recording and the file so far is kept ("Stopped, the file so far is kept"); turning it on starts recording on this device. Where recording is not possible, the row shows "Recording: Not available", and on some devices the app says "Cannot be changed during a broadcast on this device".
  • If your MAXCOM Kiosk Box has not been updated yet, the Screen switch is shown on and cannot be changed, with the note "Update the MAXCOM Kiosk Box for this choice". The live still runs as usual.

A live whose video is taken by the MAXCOM Kiosk Box

When Video is taken by is MAXCOM Kiosk Box, the Box reads the external camera itself, and your phone or iPad only controls the live.

  • The live runs until someone taps Stop. It does not end by itself when the phone or iPad is locked, when you leave the app, or when the phone loses its network. It also does not end when the camera drops: the TVs show the waiting card, and the picture and sound come back by themselves when the camera connects again. When the kuliah is over, remember to tap Stop.
  • Who may tap Stop: the person who started the live, other users who have live control (the Live Broadcast role), and an Admin. If someone else stops it, the app asks "This broadcast was started by (name) from (device). Stop it?" and both names are written to the change log.
  • If the device that started the live has not been seen for a few minutes, other devices that open the app show the notice "The broadcast is still running, no device is in control" with a Stop the broadcast button. The broadcast to the TVs and YouTube goes on.
  • The phone and the iPad show a control card, not the video: "The picture is not shown on this device. Watch it on the TV or on YouTube." The card shows On air, the broadcast time, who started it, how many TVs are showing the live, the state of YouTube and of the camera, and the sound levels. One still picture is taken only when you open Share the link.
  • Recording: an iPad keeps recording on the iPad (the row "Recording on this iPad"). An Android phone records only when This phone takes the video; when the Box takes the video, recording on the phone is not available.
  • If you leave the app during a live, the reminder says "The broadcast is carried on by MAXCOM. Open the app again to control or stop it."

Sound when the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video

  • The main sound is the MAXCOM microphone (main), the microphone connected to the Box (mic-in), at volume 100. Only one sound is on at a time.
  • This microphone has its own sound path on the Box, so the sound stays smooth when the camera or the network stutters.
  • The camera sound (the row External camera audio) is listed but is not on air. To use the sound of the hall instead, turn that row on: the MAXCOM microphone is then turned off.
  • MAXCOM joins the sound to the picture with a fixed delay. There is nothing to set for the sound delay.

Automatic quality on YouTube

When the upload to YouTube is slow, MAXCOM lowers the picture quality sent to YouTube (480p) and returns to the normal quality by itself when the internet recovers. On an Android MAXCOM Kiosk Box the quality is not lowered: the broadcast is kept in a buffer and the notice "Upload to YouTube is slow" is shown.

Show the live on each TV

In Live settings, the card Show the live on this TV has one switch for each Box, on by default. Turn it off for a Box that is slow during a live: its TV keeps the normal display and the live is not touched.

Notices you may see

What you seeWhat it means and what to do
"The camera picture is slow"A camera app on a phone or iPad becomes a second reader, and two readers drop the picture to 4 to 6 frames a second. Close the camera app during the broadcast.
"No sound from the MAXCOM microphone"The microphone on the Box sends no sound. Check the microphone cable in the MAXCOM mic-in socket, or tap Use the camera sound. After about 90 seconds of silence the camera sound takes over by itself and the notice becomes "Camera sound in use".
"The MAXCOM microphone sound is breaking up"Check that the microphone cable is firm, or turn on the row External camera audio.
"The MAXCOM microphone is paused for now, the camera sound is used"The microphone on the Box stalled or was unplugged. An unplugged microphone is noticed within about 10 seconds and the camera sound is used until it returns. The broadcast is not silent. The microphone is tried again by itself, after 1 minute and then after longer waits (up to 5 minutes).
"The MAXCOM microphone is not detected"The broadcast may be silent. Check the microphone connection on the MAXCOM Kiosk Box.
"Upload to YouTube is slow" or "Picture quality lowered (480p)"The upload from the musalla to YouTube is slow. The broadcast carries on and the quality returns by itself. If it happens every time, check the internet at the musalla.
"The connection to YouTube was resumed n times"MAXCOM connected to YouTube again by itself. Information only.
"The camera is disconnected, the broadcast goes on"The TVs show the waiting card. The picture and sound return by themselves when the camera connects again. The broadcast does not stop by itself; tap Stop when the kuliah is over. What YouTube viewers see in the meantime is decided by YouTube.
"The broadcast is still running, no device is in control"The device that started the live has not been seen for a few minutes. The broadcast goes on. Tap Stop the broadcast when the kuliah is over.

Tips for a steady broadcast

  • Connect the MAXCOM Kiosk Box with a LAN cable where possible, not Wi-Fi.
  • Give the camera steady power. An unsteady supply makes the camera drop.
  • Close the camera maker's app on every phone and tablet during a live.

Share the live link on WhatsApp

While a live goes to YouTube, the Dashboard notice "The live is running." with the button Share the link appears on the Dashboard and on the Live broadcast page.

  1. Tap Share the link. The Live stream notice sheet opens with a ready message: kuliah, speaker, book and the YouTube link. To change the text tap Edit text, then Done.
  2. Tap the main button. Share to WhatsApp opens WhatsApp on a phone that has it, and you choose the groups. On a phone without WhatsApp it is Share, and on an iPad or computer it is Send from a phone: scan the large QR code with any phone to open WhatsApp there. Copy text copies the text.

If the YouTube broadcast was cut, the full recording can be uploaded later in YouTube upload.

5. What you cannot do

Ask an Admin for these (the app does not show them, or shows them for viewing only):

  • The musalla settings in Configuration (you can only view them). You can sign in to YouTube yourself on the musalla Wi-Fi, but normally an Admin does the first sign-in. If the app says "YouTube is not connected yet", sign in on the musalla Wi-Fi or ask an Admin.
  • Add or change kuliah, posters or other content, or see Finance.
  • Manage users and devices.
  • Use the camera settings or the YouTube sign-in away from the musalla: they work on the musalla Wi-Fi only (the camera address, user name and password can be sent from away).

6. If something is wrong

  • I cannot see the Live broadcast menu: the menu depends on your role and on whether live is on. Ask an Admin to check your roles.
  • The broadcast does not reach YouTube: check the YouTube switch in the Broadcast destinations sheet (tap the chips above the start button). If it is off, YouTube is not chosen; it can be turned on during a live. "YouTube needs to be connected again" means an Admin or you must sign in again on the musalla Wi-Fi. The broadcast to the musalla screen keeps running.
  • "YouTube: cannot be checked right now" is not a sign-out. Tap the cloud icon and Sync now, wait a minute, and do not sign in again.
  • The camera Test connection fails: read the reason (the camera cannot be reached, a wrong user name or password, the address was not found, or the codec is not supported; use H.264).
  • A new microphone is not picked up: plug it into the device that is broadcasting, check the microphone permission, then unplug and plug it in again.
  • My phone has no WhatsApp: use Share, or Send from a phone and scan with a phone that has it.
  • The enrolment code has expired: ask the Admin for a new code.
  • I forgot my PIN, or the PIN is locked: see section 3, or ask an Admin to unlock you in Users & devices.
  • My phone is lost: tell an Admin at once. The Admin deletes that device so it can no longer unlock.
  • My change is not on the TV yet: look at the cloud and box icons in the top bar. If changes are waiting, tap Sync now. From home it can take a few minutes.
  • My phone was locked or the app was closed during a live: if the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video, the live goes on. Open the app and tap Stop when the kuliah is over. If the camera of the phone or iPad itself is used, the live stops because the camera stops.
  • I do not see the video on my phone: that is right when the MAXCOM Kiosk Box takes the video. Watch it on the TV or on YouTube.
  • Go live cannot be tapped: no destination is on. Turn on Screen, YouTube or Record in the Broadcast destinations sheet.
  • The picture is slow or stalls: close the camera app, and check the network cable and the power of the camera.
  • Still stuck: write to admin@mymaxcom.my.
