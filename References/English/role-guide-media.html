MAXCOM R2, role guide

Role guide: Media

For the person who looks after what the TV shows at the musalla, for example the media volunteer of Surau Contoh. You add kuliah, speakers, books, posters and announcements. The full steps are in the user manual, Chapters 4 and 5.

1. What this role is for

The Media role is for Content and posters. You see the Dashboard and the Content menu, and you can add, edit and delete what the TV shows. You can look at Configuration but not change it. All other menus are hidden from you. Your phone can also preview the TV: tap See the TV screen on the Dashboard.

2. Join with the QR code

You join the musalla on your own phone. You join with a QR code made by an Admin of Surau Contoh (in Bahasa Melayu the button is Daftar peranti).

  1. Ask an Admin for a QR code. The Admin makes it in Users & devices on the musalla Wi-Fi. A code is valid for 10 minutes by default; if it has expired, ask for a new one.
  2. Install the MAX Admin Client on your phone (download it from mymaxcom.my, see Chapter 2.1 of the user manual) and open it. Choose the language with BM | EN | ع if you need to.
  3. Tap Scan the QR code and scan the code on the Admin's phone. If the camera cannot be used, tap Enter the code manually and paste the enrolment code.
  4. Check your name and the musalla name (for example Surau Contoh) on the Check step.
  5. Fill in Device name, for example "Ustaz Contoh's phone".
  6. Set a PIN if the app asks for one, then tap Enrol device. The app shows "Device enrolled" and then the Dashboard.

3. Open the app

  • The App lock is set by an Admin. When it is off (the usual case), MAXCOM opens straight away on your phone. When it is on, you set a 6-digit PIN when you enrol (not all the same digit and not a sequence), and the app asks for it again after 2 hours unused, or after 12 hours. On Android a fingerprint can be used.
  • To change the PIN: More > This device > Change PIN. If you forgot it, tap Forgot your PIN? and ask an Admin to reset it (Reset PIN).
  • After several wrong PINs the app shows "PIN locked for now". Wait for the time shown, or ask an Admin to unlock you.
  • The first screen is the Dashboard. The bottom bar shows your main menus and the others are under More. A menu you have no permission for is not shown.

4. Content: your screens

All TV content is in Content. Choose a kind of content, then tap Add. The steps are the same for every kind: fill in the form and tap Save changes. To edit, tap an item in the list. To delete, open the item and tap Delete.

A deleted item leaves the TV at once and cannot be brought back.

Add a kuliah (speaker and book first)

  1. Content > Speakers > Add speaker: fill in Name, and if you like Title, Phone and Picture. Tap Save changes. The phone number is for the committee only and is not shown on the TV.
  2. Content > Books > Add book: fill in Book title, and if you like Author and Book cover. Tap Save changes.
  3. Content > Lectures > Add lecture: choose the Date and After prayer (for example Maghrib), or a Time. Fill in the Lecture name, for example "Kuliah Maghrib". Choose the Speaker and Book. Fill in the Topic or the Series if there is one. Tap Save changes.
  4. To cancel a kuliah, open it and change the Status to Cancelled. It is marked on the TV and not deleted.

Poster

Fill in Poster title, choose the Poster picture, the Days shown, Start date and End date, then tap Save changes. Order sets the turn. Pictures are made smaller before upload. Stretch picture: Yes fills the space and the shape may change; No shows the whole picture.

Announcement

Fill in Title, Announcement text, Start date, End date and Priority (Normal, Important or Urgent). The Picture is optional. An Urgent announcement is shown first on the TV, so use it only for things that really cannot wait.

Video

Content > Video > Add. Choose the Video source: Upload (up to 300 MB and 10 minutes, on the musalla Wi-Fi only) or YouTube (paste the link and the Estimated length (minutes)) or Recording. Tap Preview to see how it looks on the TV, wait for any upload to finish, then tap Save changes.

5. What you cannot do

Ask an Admin for these (the app does not show them, or shows them for viewing only):

  • Turn TV cards on or off, change their order or look in Configuration, or any other setting (you can only view Configuration).
  • Change the TV theme or the prayer time data.
  • See or record Finance, or manage users and devices.
  • Go live: the Live broadcast menu belongs to the Live role.

6. If something is wrong

  • I cannot see the Content menu: ask an Admin to check your roles.
  • A YouTube video does not play on the TV: the musalla needs internet; without it the video is skipped. Use an ordinary video link, not a live link.
  • An upload stops: videos can be uploaded on the musalla Wi-Fi only. Wait until the upload shows "Uploaded to the box" before you save.
  • I deleted something by mistake: it cannot be brought back. Add it again.
  • The enrolment code has expired: ask the Admin for a new code.
  • I forgot my PIN, or the PIN is locked: see section 3, or ask an Admin to unlock you in Users & devices.
  • My phone is lost: tell an Admin at once. The Admin deletes that device so it can no longer unlock.
  • My change is not on the TV yet: look at the cloud and box icons in the top bar. If changes are waiting, tap Sync now. From home it can take a few minutes.
  • Still stuck: write to admin@mymaxcom.my.
